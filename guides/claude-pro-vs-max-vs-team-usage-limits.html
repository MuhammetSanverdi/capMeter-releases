Claude Pro vs Max vs Ultra vs Team (Standard & Premium Seats): Usage Limits

Guide · updated 2026-11-11

Anthropic doesn't publish a fixed "X messages per day" number for any plan — usage cost depends on model, context length, and what you're doing, so a flat count would be misleading the moment it was written. What they do publish, and what actually explains the difference between plans, is a multiplier. Here's each one, quoted directly from Anthropic's own pricing page.

Two recent changes are reflected above. Anthropic renamed Max 20x to Ultra at the same price, and the pricing page now lists Max simply as "Max" at $100 a month. Max and Ultra are billed monthly only.

What's actually different, mechanically

Pro and Max use the same two-window structure: a session limit that resets every 5 hours, and a weekly limit layered on top of it. Ultra is the exception: verified Ultra accounts have no 5-hour limit, only the weekly one. Fable also has its own rules: on Pro it is paid with usage credits, while Max and Ultra include it up to half of the weekly limit.

Claude.ai chat, Claude Code, and Cowork draw from your account's limits on every plan — Anthropic describes usage limits as shared across your Claude account. Claude Code's weekly limit has had its own adjustment, though: since September 14, 2026 it is 25% higher than before Anthropic's summer promotion. (Codex has used a budget separate from ChatGPT chat, but OpenAI is merging them in December — see Claude Code vs Codex.)

Team seats: individual limits, not a shared pool

The one place people usually guess wrong: a Team workspace does not pool usage across members. Each seat has its own limit, sized by whether it's a standard or premium seat, and one person maxing out their session doesn't take capacity away from anyone else on the team. What is shared within a single seat is the same as everywhere else — chat, Claude Code, and Cowork on that one account all draw from that one seat's allowance.

Team premium seat vs Max

This is the comparison people search for most, because the prices line up: a premium Team seat is $100/seat/month billed annually ($125 monthly), and Max is $100/month. They are not the same allowance, though, and Anthropic doesn't publish a number that lets you equate them. Max is defined against Pro ("5x Pro usage every 5 hours and every week"); a premium seat is defined against a standard Team seat ("5x more usage than standard seats"), and the standard seat itself is only described as "more usage than Pro," with no exact multiplier. So a premium seat is at least as large as Max, but how much larger isn't stated anywhere official.

In practice the deciding factor is rarely the multiplier. A premium seat only exists inside a Team workspace, which needs at least two seats and brings shared billing and admin controls. If you're one person, Max gets you the same tier of usage without paying for a second seat. If you're already on Team, a premium seat is how you give your heaviest users more room without moving everyone up.

Which one to actually pick

Pro is enough for occasional-to-regular use where you're not running Claude Code continuously. Max exists specifically for the person who hits Pro's ceiling most days. Ultra is for the person running Claude Code most of the day, or whose long agent runs keep stopping at the 5-hour window rather than the weekly one. Team only makes sense once billing needs to be centralized across more than one person; picking it purely for "more usage" for a single user costs more than Max for less capacity, since Team's per-seat multiplier applies to Pro, not to Max's tiers.
