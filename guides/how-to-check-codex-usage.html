How to Check Your Codex (ChatGPT) Usage on Mac

Guide · updated 2026-09-15

Codex has two separate places that show usage, and they don't show the same thing — mixing them up is the most common source of confusion. There's also a third option if you don't want to go looking for the number at all.

Inside the Codex CLI

Two different slash commands, two different scopes. /status reports on the session you're in right now — active model, approval mode, how much context is left in this conversation. /usage is the one that actually answers "how close am I to my limit": it opens a menu for your daily, weekly, or cumulative token activity against your ChatGPT plan. If you only ever check /status, you're looking at the wrong number.

On the ChatGPT usage page

Sign in at chatgpt.com and go to Settings → Usage. This shows your rolling 5-hour window and weekly window as a percentage, plus the reset time for each. It's the same account-level data Codex CLI's /usage is reading — just in a browser instead of a terminal, and you have to remember to open it. (If those two windows themselves are confusing — why there are two, why they don't reset together — this covers just that. And if that screen shows a reset you didn't expect, it's probably a banked reset — Codex actually has one of those, unlike Claude.)

Without having to ask

Both of the above share the same gap: you see the number only when you go check it. A menu bar app can keep it visible continuously instead. CapMeter reads your Codex session the same way it reads Claude's — as an account-level percentage with a live countdown — and shows both providers in the same popup if you use both. If you use Codex and Claude side by side, that's the actual point: one ring in the menu bar instead of two apps, or one app that only covers half of what you use.
