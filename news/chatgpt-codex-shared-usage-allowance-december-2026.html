ChatGPT and Codex will share one usage allowance starting in December

OpenAI says that starting in December, the usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business plans will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex, and measured in tokens.

Published 2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-11Source: Not dated exactly; the Help Center article showed "Updated: 10 days ago" on 2026-11-11

What changes

OpenAI's Help Center says that from December, the usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business plans will be shared between ChatGPT and Codex. OpenAI ties the change to bringing the agentic features of ChatGPT Work into the main ChatGPT experience.

Usage will be counted in tokens. How fast the allowance runs down depends on the model and the task: OpenAI says more advanced models and more complex work generally use more of it. Instant chat will not count against the allowance.

Who it affects

Plus, Pro and Business plans, according to the Help Center article. It does not mention Free, Go, Enterprise or Edu.

What it means for your limits

Today, OpenAI's Codex pricing documentation says Codex shares its usage with ChatGPT Work. After this change, the allowance will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex as a whole, so heavy ChatGPT use on advanced models can leave less room for Codex, and the other way round. Instant chat is the stated exception.

OpenAI says it expects most users will not have to wait for a reset more often than they do now. Combined remaining usage and reset times will appear in Settings → Usage. When the allowance runs out, the options OpenAI lists are waiting for the reset, upgrading, or buying credits.

What the source does not say

The article gives no exact date in December, no size for the shared allowance, and no per-plan figures. It also does not say whether reset schedules will change.
