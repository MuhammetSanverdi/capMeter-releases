Claude Code weekly limits are now permanently 25% higher

Anthropic's temporary 50% boost to Claude Code weekly limits ended on September 13, 2026. Part of it stayed: since September 14, weekly limits in Claude Code are 25% higher than before the promotion.

Published 2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-11Source: Effective 2026-09-14, as stated by the source

What changed

From May 13 to September 13, 2026, a promotion raised the weekly usage limit in Claude Code by 50%. Anthropic's Help Center says it had planned to return weekly limits to their original level afterwards, but decided to keep part of the increase.

Since September 14, 2026, weekly limits in Claude Code are 25% higher than they were before the promotion.

Who it affects

The permanent increase applies to Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and seat-based Enterprise plans. The original promotion did not include Free plans or consumption-based Enterprise seats.

What it means for your limits

The change is to the weekly limit only. The promotion did not affect five-hour limits, and the article does not describe any change to them now. The promotion applied to Claude Code wherever you use it (CLI, IDE extensions, desktop and web) and did not change limits for Claude chat or Cowork.

You can see your current Claude Code limits with /usage in the CLI.

What the source does not say

The article does not give an absolute size for the weekly limit, only the change relative to before the promotion. It also does not say whether the 25% increase is guaranteed for a fixed period.
