Claude Fable 5.2 launch: a free Fable week for Pro and up to three Fable resets for Max and Ultra

To launch Claude Fable 5.2 on November 9, 2026, Anthropic is giving paid plans extra Fable usage: Pro plans get Fable models included for one week plus one Fable reset, and Max and Ultra plans get one to three Fable resets depending on how long they have subscribed.

Published 2026-11-15Updated 2026-11-15Source: Offers start 2026-11-09, as stated by the source; the page itself showed "Updated this week" on 2026-11-15

What changed

Anthropic's Help Center describes two temporary offers tied to the launch of Fable 5.2 on November 9, 2026. Both cover every Fable model: Fable 5.2, Fable 5.1 and Fable 5.

  • Pro plans and standard Team seats: Fable models are included in the plan's weekly limits from November 9 until November 16, 2026 at 11:59 pm PT. During that week you can spend up to 50% of your weekly limit on Fable without paying extra. You also get one Fable limit reset, which expires at the same time.
  • Max, Ultra, premium Team seats and premium seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans: one, two or three Fable limit resets, depending on how long you have been a paying subscriber. These expire on March 8, 2027 at 11:59 pm PT.

How many resets you get

  • Less than six months as a paying subscriber: one reset
  • Six to 12 months: two resets
  • More than 12 months: three resets

Subscription length counts from your first paid bill (from your first app store bill if you subscribed through one), even if you canceled and came back later. On Team and Enterprise plans it counts from the organization's first paid bill, so every premium seat in the organization gets the same number.

Who it affects

The offers apply to Pro, Max and Ultra plans, standard and premium seats on Team plans, and premium seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans. They do not apply to the Free plan, standard seats on legacy seat-based Enterprise plans, self-serve Enterprise plans, usage-based Enterprise plans or HIPAA-ready Enterprise plans.

Resets only go to accounts whose paid billing was in place before November 9, 2026 at 10:00 am PT. Team and Enterprise seat holders must also have joined their organization before then. A subscription still in a free trial at that time gets its resets once it converts to paid.

What it means for your limits

A Fable limit reset does not raise any limit. On the weekly limit, it gives back only the usage you spent on Fable models; usage on other models stays where it is. If your account has a five-hour limit, the reset refills that window completely, including usage on other models. The Help Center's example: if you had used 80% of your weekly limit, 50% on Fable and 30% on other models, a Fable reset takes you back to 30%.

Verified Ultra accounts have no five-hour limit, so for them a reset only affects the weekly limit.

For Pro plans, Fable models draw on the weekly limit faster than other Claude models, and the included week has a cap of 50% of the weekly limit. Once that 50% is used, or once the week ends, Fable usage is billed to usage credits if you have them turned on. Unused allowance does not carry over. Your default model does not change; Fable has to be picked in the model selector.

Resets are used from Settings → Usage → Limit resets → "Reset for free", at any time before they expire. Once used, a reset cannot be undone, and a reset you have not used is lost if you cancel or downgrade.

What the source does not say

The article does not say whether a similar included week or more resets will follow later, and it does not give the size of the weekly limit in absolute terms. The page carries no exact publication date; the dates above are the offer dates the page states.
