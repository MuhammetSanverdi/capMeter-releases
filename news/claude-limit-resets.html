Claude limit resets: one-time refills for your five-hour or weekly limit

Anthropic occasionally gives eligible Claude plans a one-time "limit reset". Using it immediately refills your five-hour limit, your weekly limit, or the weekly usage you spent on Fable, depending on the type.

Published 2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-11Source: Not dated on the source page

What a limit reset does

Anthropic's Help Center describes three kinds:

  • Five-hour reset: your five-hour session limit is refilled immediately.
  • Weekly reset: your weekly limit is refilled immediately.
  • Fable limit reset: gives back the weekly usage you spent on Fable models; usage on other models stays as it is. If your account has a five-hour limit, that is refilled in full too.

Your weekly limit still resets on its normal day and time afterwards.

How to use one

In Claude on the web, the desktop app or the mobile apps, open Settings → Usage and choose "Reset for free" under Limit resets. The same button appears in the message shown when you reach a limit.

It is not available inside Claude Code in the terminal or IDE. Because limits are shared across your Claude account, a reset used elsewhere also gives you the usage back in Claude Code.

Rules worth knowing

  • You do not have to wait until you hit a limit to use it, and once used it cannot be undone.
  • Unused resets can expire; the expiry is shown in Settings → Usage.
  • A reset does not refund extra usage you were already billed for and does not change your usage credit balance.
  • If you downgrade or cancel before using it, you lose it.

What the source does not say

The article says resets are given "occasionally" to eligible plans, but does not say which plans are eligible, how often resets are given, or when the feature started.
