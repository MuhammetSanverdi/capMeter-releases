Claude's Max 20x plan is now called Ultra

Anthropic has renamed the Max 20x plan to Ultra. Existing Max 20x subscribers move to Ultra automatically with no price change, and verified Ultra accounts have no five-hour limit.

Published 2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-11Source: Not dated on the source page

What changed

According to Anthropic's Help Center, the plan previously called Max 20x is now called Ultra. Existing Max 20x subscribers are moved to Ultra automatically and pay the same price.

Ultra costs $200 a month for web subscriptions; mobile pricing may differ by app platform. It is currently available as a monthly subscription only.

How Ultra's limits work

  • Weekly allowance: 10 times the Pro plan's usage every week.
  • No five-hour limit for verified accounts. Accounts that are not yet verified have a temporary limit until they are.
  • Two weekly limits: one across all models, and a separate weekly limit for Fable.
  • Anthropic says further limits may apply to manage capacity, such as weekly or monthly caps or limits on specific models or features.

The plan also includes Claude Code and monthly Claude API credits.

What it means for your limits

If you were on Max 20x, nothing about your billing changes. What may change is how you hit the limit: with no five-hour window on a verified account, the weekly limit (and, on Fable, the separate Fable limit) is the one to watch.

What the source does not say

The Help Center page does not give a date for the rename. The old name described the plan as 20 times Pro; the Ultra page describes 10 times Pro usage per week. The page does not explain how those two figures relate, so we do not compare them.
