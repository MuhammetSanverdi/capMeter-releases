October 7, 2026: some Claude organizations were wrongly paused at their spend limit

On October 7, 2026, Anthropic's status page recorded an issue in which some organizations were incorrectly paused for having reached their spend limit. Requests were refused across several Claude products until it was resolved.

Published 2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-11Source: 2026-10-07, 21:23 UTC (resolution posted on the status page)

What happened

According to Anthropic's status page, an issue caused some organizations to be paused as if they had reached their spend limit when they had not. Their requests were refused on the Claude API, Claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Security and Claude Cowork. The incident is marked resolved.

Who it affected

"Some organizations", in the status page's words. It does not say which plans or how many.

What it means

If you saw requests refused for a spend limit on October 7, 2026 while you were below your limit, this incident is the recorded explanation: the status page describes it as an issue on Anthropic's side, not a limit you reached.

What the source does not say

The incident page shows only the resolution update, posted at 21:23 UTC. It does not give a start time, a duration or a cause.
