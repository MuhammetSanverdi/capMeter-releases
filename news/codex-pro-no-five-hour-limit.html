Codex on ChatGPT Pro currently has no five-hour limit

OpenAI's Codex pricing documentation says Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit. Plus and Standard Business plans still have a five-hour allowance, and weekly limits may also apply.

Published 2026-11-11Updated 2026-11-11Source: Not dated on the source page

What the documentation says

In its answer to "What are the usage limits for my plan?", OpenAI's Codex pricing page publishes estimates of local messages per five-hour period for Plus and Standard Business, and states that Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit.

The estimates are wide ranges, not fixed caps. For example, the page lists 15–160 local messages per five hours on GPT-6.1 Sol and 350–3,000 on GPT-6 Luna, for both Plus and Standard Business. OpenAI notes that cloud tasks can use more of the allowance than local messages, that local messages and cloud chats share one allowance, and that weekly limits may also apply.

Who it affects

ChatGPT Pro, which the page lists at $100, $200 or $500 per month. Ultrafast mode is listed for the $500 tier.

What it means for your limits

On Pro, the short rolling window is not what stops you, according to this page. The page does not say Pro has no limits at all: weekly limits may apply, and Fast and Ultrafast modes use the included allowance faster (2.5x and 8x relative to Standard).

When a Plus or Pro user reaches the limit, the page says they can buy additional credits. Any user can also run extra local chats with an API key, billed at API rates.

What the source does not say

The page says "currently" and gives no date for when Pro's five-hour limit was removed or whether that will change. It does not publish Pro's weekly limits.
