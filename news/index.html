Claude & Codex limit changes

Dated records of changes to Claude and Codex usage limits, plans and pricing, each linked to the official source. How we write these · RSS

Codex2026-11-11

ChatGPT and Codex will share one usage allowance starting in December

From December, Plus, Pro and Business plans will draw ChatGPT and Codex usage from one shared, token-based allowance. OpenAI has not given an exact date or size.

Codex2026-11-11

Codex on ChatGPT Pro currently has no five-hour limit

OpenAI's Codex pricing documentation says Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit. Plus and Standard Business still have one, and weekly limits may apply.

Claude2026-11-11

Claude's Max 20x plan is now called Ultra

Max 20x has been renamed Ultra at the same $200 a month. Verified Ultra accounts have no five-hour limit and get 10 times Pro usage every week.

Claude2026-11-11

Claude Code weekly limits are now permanently 25% higher

Anthropic's 50% Claude Code weekly limit promotion ended on September 13, 2026. Since September 14, weekly limits are 25% above the pre-promotion level.

Claude2026-11-11

Claude limit resets: one-time refills for your five-hour or weekly limit

Eligible Claude plans occasionally get a one-time limit reset that refills the five-hour limit, the weekly limit or weekly Fable usage, used from Settings → Usage.

Claude2026-11-11

October 7, 2026: some Claude organizations were wrongly paused at their spend limit

Anthropic's status page recorded an issue that wrongly paused some organizations for reaching their spend limit, refusing requests across the API, Claude.ai and Claude Code.
