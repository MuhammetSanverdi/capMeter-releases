Claude & Codex limit changes

Dated records of changes to Claude and Codex usage limits, plans and pricing, each linked to the official source. How we write these · RSS

Claude2026-11-15

Claude Fable 5.2 launch: a free Fable week for Pro and up to three Fable resets for Max and Ultra

From November 9 to 16, 2026, Pro plans can spend up to 50% of their weekly limit on Fable models. Max and Ultra get one to three Fable limit resets, valid until March 8, 2027.

Codex2026-11-11

Plus, Pro and Business: Codex and ChatGPT move to one token budget in December

OpenAI plans to count Codex and ChatGPT usage on Plus, Pro and Business against a single token-based budget from December. Instant chat is excluded; no exact date or size yet.

Codex2026-11-11

Codex on ChatGPT Pro currently has no five-hour limit

OpenAI's Codex pricing documentation says Pro plans currently have no five-hour limit. Plus and Standard Business still have one, and weekly limits may apply.

Claude2026-11-11

Ultra replaces Max 20x: same $200, and only weekly caps on verified accounts

Anthropic moved Max 20x subscribers to a plan called Ultra at $200 a month. Verified Ultra accounts have weekly caps only (10× Pro, plus a Fable cap) and no five-hour window.

Claude2026-11-11

Claude Code's summer boost ended, but half of it stayed for good

The +50% Claude Code weekly boost ran May 13 – September 13, 2026. From September 14 Anthropic kept +25% over the pre-May level on Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and seat-based Enterprise.

Claude2026-11-11

Claude limit resets: one-time refills for your five-hour or weekly limit

Eligible Claude plans occasionally get a one-time limit reset that refills the five-hour limit, the weekly limit or weekly Fable usage, used from Settings → Usage.

Claude2026-11-11

October 7, 2026: some Claude organizations were wrongly paused at their spend limit

Anthropic's status page recorded an issue that wrongly paused some organizations for reaching their spend limit, refusing requests across the API, Claude.ai and Claude Code.
