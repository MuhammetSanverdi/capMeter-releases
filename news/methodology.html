How we write these records

Updated 2026-11-11

CapMeter publishes short, dated records of changes to Claude and Codex usage limits, plans and pricing. This page explains how they are made, so you can decide how much to rely on them.

What we cover

Only Claude (Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI), the two services CapMeter tracks. Within those: changes to usage limits, plan names and prices, limit-related features, and service incidents that the provider recorded on its official status page. We do not cover every announcement, and silence here does not mean nothing changed.

Sources

Every record is based on primary sources only: the providers' help centers, pricing pages, documentation and status pages. Posts on Reddit, X or forums are not a source. If something is only rumored, we do not publish it.

Each record lists its sources and the date we last checked them. Many official pages are not dated, or show only a relative date such as "updated 10 days ago". In that case the record says so instead of guessing a date.

Three different dates

  • Published: when we first published the record.
  • Updated: the last time we changed it.
  • Source: the date the provider gives for the change itself, or a note that the source gives none.

Some records were published after the change happened, so the published date can be later than the source date.

How records are written

Drafts may be prepared with the help of an AI model. Before publishing, every statement in a record is checked by a person against the source it cites. Anything we cannot match to the source is removed or labeled as not stated. Records summarize the source in our own words and link to it; the provider remains the authority on your actual plan and limits, which can vary by account and region.

Corrections

When a source changes or we find a mistake, we update the record, move its updated date forward and add a note under "Corrections" saying what changed. We do not silently rewrite records.

Conflict of interest

These records are published by the maker of CapMeter, a paid Mac app that shows Claude and Codex usage. Product information is kept to a clearly labeled "From the publisher" box at the end of each record and never appears in the record itself. CapMeter is not affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI.
