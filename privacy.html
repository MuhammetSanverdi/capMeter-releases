Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

CapMeter ("the app") is a macOS menu bar utility that displays your personal Claude.ai usage statistics. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect your personal data, and does not send any of your data to us or to any analytics, advertising, or tracking service. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No crash reporting sent externally
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data about you sent to us — there is no backend that receives it
  • No account credentials stored outside your device

The app sends nothing about you to us or to any analytics service. Three network behaviours are worth naming explicitly, because each one is either your own doing or Claude's:

  • If you set up a webhook yourself, the app sends usage alerts to the address you provide — described under "Network Requests" below.
  • Alerts posted to Slack or Discord carry the app's icon, which is hosted on capmeter.app — a static marketing site with no database and no account system. When those services render the message, their servers fetch that image; nothing about you is sent with it, and the app never contacts capmeter.app itself.
  • When you add an account, the app opens Claude.ai's own sign-in page in a window inside the app. That page is served by Anthropic and may contact the services it normally uses — for example Google Sign-In, hCaptcha or Cloudflare — exactly as it would in your browser. Any cookies they set are stored in the app's isolated session on your Mac. "No analytics or tracking" above means the app contains no such code of its own; it does not change how Claude.ai's own sign-in page behaves.

Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Account information — For each Claude account you add, the app stores its session token (cookie), account name, and email address locally, so you can switch between accounts. Session tokens are encrypted with the macOS Keychain before being written to disk, whenever Keychain encryption is available on your Mac, and the app's settings file is readable only by your user account. Browser sessions are kept isolated in the app's own sandboxed session.
  • Usage history — A local log of your past usage, stored in the app's sandboxed container and used to draw the history charts. Each entry holds the date and time, the time your session resets, your session and weekly usage percentages, your plan name, the associated account email, and — if you use Claude's extra usage credits — the amount spent, which is what the spend-rate figure is calculated from.
  • App preferences — Notification settings, refresh interval, display options, and any webhook addresses you configure. Stored locally via Electron's app data storage.
  • Licence state — The date your trial started and whether the one-time purchase has been made, kept in two small local files. These contain no personal information.

Network Requests

The app communicates with claude.ai on your behalf, using your own authenticated session, to retrieve your usage data. Requests are made to:

  • claude.ai/api/organizations — to identify which organization your account belongs to
  • claude.ai/api/organizations/…/usage — to retrieve your usage limits
  • claude.ai/api/account — to retrieve your account name and plan

If you add more than one account, the accounts you are not currently viewing are refreshed in the background roughly every four hours, using each one's own session, so their history stays complete. The same three endpoints above are used.

The app never reads, stores, or transmits your Claude conversations.

The app does not send your data to us or any analytics service. The only other network destination is optional and entirely under your control: if you configure one or more webhook addresses (Slack, Discord, or any HTTP endpoint) in the app's settings, the app sends an alert to each of them when you cross a usage threshold, when your session renews, and when you press the test button. Slack and Discord alerts contain your usage percentage, your Claude account email address, your plan name, and your reset time; alerts to a plain HTTP endpoint omit the email address. This feature is disabled by default, is sent only to the addresses you provide, and is only active if you set it up. You can remove them at any time in settings to stop it.

Purchases

CapMeter offers a single one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive or store your payment details. The app only records locally whether the purchase has been made.

Your Control

You can remove all locally stored data at any time by deleting the app together with its data folder at ~/Library/Containers/com.gulizarsanverdi.capmeter/. Removing an account inside the app deletes it from your settings and clears the active session cookie; deleting that folder is what clears any remaining Claude.ai cookies. One item lives outside that folder: the key used to encrypt your session tokens is held in your macOS Keychain and can be removed there — it is a key, not data about you.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy is updated here and the date at the top changes with it. Because the app stores everything on your Mac and sends nothing to us, changes will normally be clarifications rather than new data practices.
