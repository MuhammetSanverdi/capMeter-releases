Claude Burn-Rate Calculator

Free tool · updated 2026-09-16

Two numbers tell you more than the percentage alone: how much you've used, and how long it took. Enter both and this works out your burn rate, then tells you whether you'll hit your limit before the 5-hour window resets on its own — the same math CapMeter runs automatically in your menu bar.

The math, if you want to check it

Burn rate = usage % ÷ minutes elapsed. Divide the remaining percentage (100 − current) by that rate to get minutes left at the current pace. Compare that to what's left in the 5-hour window (300 minutes − minutes elapsed) — whichever runs out first is what actually happens to you.

What this doesn't account for

This assumes a steady pace for the rest of the window — a long agent run or a sudden burst of heavy prompts moves the estimate a lot, in either direction. It also only covers the session window; there's a separate weekly cap layered on top that this calculator doesn't touch, and its reset timing is less exact than it looks.
